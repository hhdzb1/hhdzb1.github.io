    I am Evan Brooks (Yi Ba), an undergraduate researcher in Automotive Engineering at Wuhan University of Technology. I am currently a research intern at Shanghai Jiao Tong University with Prof. Tong Qin and an undergraduate researcher with Prof. Jing Luo.

    My research interests include robot learning, contact-rich manipulation, shared autonomy, and motion planning. My current work spans force-aware shared control on a Sawyer manipulator and dynamic navigation for quadruped robots.
