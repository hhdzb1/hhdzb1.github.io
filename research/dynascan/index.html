    DynaSCAN

    Dynamic obstacle avoidance for quadruped robots
    Shanghai Jiao Tong University · Jul 2026–Present
    Research Intern · Advisor: Prof. Tong Qin

    I am extending SCAN-Planner for the Unitree Go2 with obstacle tracking, motion prediction, and clearance-aware B-spline optimization. The goal is to make local planning consistent with sensing delay, robot body geometry, and the time needed to turn before moving.

    My contribution

    • Implemented a 5 m simulated point-cloud observation pipeline with clustering, constant-velocity Kalman filtering, and data association. The tracker estimates velocity and maintains identities from position observations, without simulator velocities, object IDs, or future trajectories.
    • Developed dynamic-clearance costs along the B-spline and automatic comparison of left/right trajectory seeds. Projected A* supplies static detour references.
    • Added an independent trajectory validator using a two-cylinder Go2 body model with explicit turning and translation timing before execution.

    Project demonstration

    Preliminary Simulation Results

    • ✓ Collision-free completion in controlled dynamic scenarios
    • ✓ Explicit robot-body collision checking
    • ✓ Minimum observed dynamic clearance: 0.217–0.288 m

    Current focus: Integrating M-detector outputs, handling prediction errors, and validating the planner on the real Unitree Go2.

    Scope of the evidence. These are small, controlled kinematic simulations. Clearance figures come from exact-future diagnostics; observation tests use simulator-labeled dynamic points without LiDAR occlusion. Reliable avoidance in cluttered scenes and on hardware remains unvalidated.
